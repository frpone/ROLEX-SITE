Chapter I

A
laboratory

Ten years of work on the crown under brand constraint. This series approaches it free. Like a painter who studies a single chair for months — same subject, hundreds of treatments, no commercial end.

Twenty-four posters, six graphic registers. Op art, photography, typography, sculpture, illustration, brutalism. One single source motif. One question : how many images can one icon yield ?

Le Studio

Where the icon is taken apart.

Pinned to the wall like working notes — the early language of the series. Typography, signs, scribbles, drafts. The crown as material, before it becomes image. Seven studies, three groups, one obsession.
